5 Budget-Friendly Destinations in South Africa

You do not need to spend a fortune for a great holiday. These five places offer great value, especially outside school holidays:

  1. Cape Town: free hikes, beaches and markets.
  2. Durban: warm sea and affordable beachfront stays.
  3. The Drakensberg: mountain views and cheap self-catering.
  4. The Garden Route: scenic drives and guest houses.
  5. Kruger National Park: budget safaris from nearby camps.

How to Pack Smart for Any Trip?

Packing well saves stress and baggage fees. Our top tips:

  • Roll your clothes to save space.
  • Keep documents and medication in your hand luggage.
  • Carry a copy of your passport.
  • Pack a charger and a power bank.

Why Book Early?

Booking flights and accommodation early usually means lower prices and more choice. Popular dates such as December and Easter sell out fast, so speak to us at least three months before you travel.

What You Need Before Travelling Overseas?

Check that your passport is valid for at least six months and has blank pages. Find out if your destination needs a visa, and arrange travel insurance. We can help you with all three.
